Place real-size pieces and check the walkways

Pick a piece and it lands on a clear spot near the middle of your view. Drag to move (5 cm steps); a face within 8 cm of a wall or piece closes up against it (hold Alt to place freely). The arrows nudge it 5 cm (Shift 1 cm), R turns it 90° (Shift+R 15°), Delete removes it, Ctrl+Z (⌘Z) undoes. Type a width and depth to size it. Drag a piece from the list straight onto the floor, and turn the chosen one with the mouse wheel. The designed furniture moves too: grab it and it comes out of the design as a trial, which Put the design back undoes. Lines show the gap on each side; under 75 cm turns amber. When you're happy, tell me and I draw the layout into the plan page and make it this plan's starting layout. Each plan keeps its own layout.

How it works

One drawing, to the centimetre. Everything here is built from the architect's drawing: every wall, door, window and room where the drawing puts it. A lock checks the measurements on every build, so nothing moves by accident.

True size. Standing in a room at 100%, things look their real size. For the closest match, set your screen and how far you sit in More, True size.

The real sun. The sun follows its true path over the site for the day and hour you pick, and the shadows fall where they would.

Trials stay trials. Finishes you try, furniture you place and designed pieces you move are kept apart from the design. Back to the design and Put the design back restore it, and Ctrl+Z (⌘Z) undoes any step.

Yours to share. What you try stays in this browser. Copy a link to this view and the next person sees the same view, finishes and furniture. Save as a picture keeps the view with its finishes named.
